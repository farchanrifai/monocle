Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: anka@pakaryan.com. Replies usually within two working days. Including your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Purchases

I bought Pro but the app still shows Free

Open Settings in Anka and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Anka. Apple handles billing and refunds; you can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my cards if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. You keep one card active; the others stay readable and come back when Pro is active again.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no Anka server. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your iCloud or computer backup restores Anka with everything else. You can also export a backup in Anka (Settings › Backup & restore), send the file to the new phone, and restore it there.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Reset data (a safety backup is saved first), or delete the app.

Chat

Why is chat unavailable?

Chat runs on Apple Intelligence on your device. It needs a supported iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, in a supported language. Right after turning it on, the model may still be downloading. Everything else in Anka works without it.

Currencies

Where do exchange rates come from?

European Central Bank daily reference rates, via Frankfurter. Offline, or for a currency without a rate, you can enter the rate yourself.
