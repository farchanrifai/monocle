Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Anka keeps your spending on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics or advertising SDK, and no Anka server. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your device

Cards, expenses, categories, budgets, bills, settings, backups and chat history are stored only on your iPhone. Home Screen widgets read a summary from the same device.

Chat runs on Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Your messages and spending are not sent anywhere for it.

The Face ID or passcode lock is handled by iOS. Anka never sees your biometrics.

The one network request

When an expense uses a currency other than your reporting currency, Anka asks Frankfurter (European Central Bank reference rates) for the exchange rate. The request contains two currency codes and a date range, for example JPY to USD for 1–13 October. Nothing about your expenses is sent. Like any web request, Frankfurter also sees your IP address. You can enter a rate yourself instead.

Purchases

Anka Pro is sold by Apple. Apple handles payment; Anka only learns from the App Store whether your Apple Account has an active purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Backups

Backups you export are files you control. Anka doesn't upload them. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup may include Anka's data, as it does for any app.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset data in Anka, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes everything.

Sharing, selling, tracking

Anka does not collect, sell or share your data and does not track you across apps or websites. Anka is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. A change that sends data off your device would be explained in the app first.
